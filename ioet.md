## Company policies
- https://www.notion.so/ioet/ioet-Policies-V-3-21653fa4fef4807bbadbc4194f4cbcac
- 40 hours per week
- No fixed login or logout time, but you must align your availability with your client and team’s working hours
- example: 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM
- Client’s working hours is a key priority
- Inform your team if you’ll be offline or unavailable during working hours
- **Do not disappear** during the workday without notifying your team
- If your client reaches out outside your usual hours, **respond as soon as possible**
- Extra Work Compensation:
	- The **client formally requests it**.
	- The **request is validated and approved** by both your **Team Lead** and you.
	- It is not intended to replace proper project planning or to normalize regular overtime.
- **speak with HR first before taking any action in BambooHR**.
- **The client always comes first.**
- **Always prioritize client meetings over internal meetings or activities.**
- we grow because we're reliable, responsive, and available when it matters
- Never leave a message from a client unanswered
- respond as soon as possible
- **Staying responsive shows we care — and it's one of the main reasons clients continue to choose ioet.**
- Communication Best Practices
	- If you don’t fully understand a task or request, politely ask the client to rephrase. Summarize it in your own words to confirm mutual understanding.
	- If you identify a potential risk, problem, or improvement opportunity, communicate it clearly and early.
		- Before sharing improvement ideas, create a brief summary or sketch of your idea.
		- Consult first with your Team Lead or, if you don’t have one assigned, with the Head of Project Operations.
		- If approved, ask the client if they’re open for some suggestions, improvements or new ideas.
		- Only elaborate once they’ve expressed interest.
	- Even in casual settings, remember that a client is a business partner, not a personal contact.
- If you’d like to request feedback from a client, coordinate this with Carlos Ruiz or your Team Lead to ensure it’s done in a structured and low-risk way.
	- **Do not proceed without consulting first.**
- We want you to succeed with your client, and you’re never expected to handle challenges alone. If something isn’t going as expected, reach out to your Team Lead. We’re here to help.
- Please **inform the Project Operations  and Account Management team immediately** if:
	- The client shares feedback (positive or negative).
	- The client plans to expand the team or launch a new project.
	- You receive notice about the **end date of a project** or staffing change.
- After important client conversations, **write a clear summary of decisions, actions, or changes discussed**.
	- Share this summary in the project’s official documentation channel (e.g., Jira, Confluence, shared drive, or **email**).
- Client's Data Handling:
	- Sharing files via personal email, USB drives, or public tools without approval is **strictly prohibited**.
	- When sharing sensitive data, **always use the company’s Secure Store app** or your official ioet company account to ensure encryption and security.
	- **Never share** internal ioet information with clients
		- Other clients or projects
		- **Internal contacts or staffing situations**
		- Company processes or strategic decisions
		- Any non-public internal updates
- If you suspect or detect a data breach, phishing attempt, or unauthorized access, **notify ioet immediately** — specifically the IT/Security team or your Team Lead.
- Excessive alcohol use or intoxication at any work-related event is not tolerated.
- ioet has three office locations: **Quito, Guayaquil, and Loja**.
	- Notify the reception and the local admin team (@Mari Zarraga in Quito, @Ana María Vivanco in Loja) in advance.
