## Onboarding
- use the same name across Gmail, Slack, Bamboo, and client systems
- you can organize your day and take breaks as needed (for errands, family, etc.).
	- notify your team if you’ll be offline during work hours
- work on weekends, holidays, or outside normal hours
	- there are specific rules for compensation (like extra days off or meal allowances)
- answer client messages promptly and stay engaged
- prepare thoroughly for client interviews, meetings, or assessments
- stay flexible with time zones
- **Never** miss a client meeting; if there’s a conflict, the client takes priority
- Lunch & Learn: informal sessions where anyone can present topics the love (lunch is company-sponsored)
- Karmas: recognize and reward teammates' great work
	- don't exaggerate points—integrity matters!
