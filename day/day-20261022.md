## Insights/Ideas
- 09:20 — `fusion_sql.py` prints its rowset parse error on a 0-row response instead of "0 rows". Rerun with `--raw`: an empty `<G_1>` in the envelope confirms zero rows.
- 09:20 — Closure cause of an AR invoice reads from the schedule columns, not the applications table: `amount_credited` non-null = closed by a credit memo application, `amount_applied` non-null = closed by cash receipt. Dev1's `ar_receivable_applications_all` can hold zero rows for both.
- 10:40 — `catalog_upload.py` takes the INNER `_datamodel.xdm` XML, not the rezipped container. Uploading the container nests it as the pod's `_datamodel.xdm` member (a zip where BIP expects XML) and breaks the DM. Symptom on re-download: the member starts with `PK\x03\x04` instead of `<?xml`. Fix: upload the inner file; the pod re-wraps it.
- 10:50 — Stage deploy tags race on busy mornings: `git ls-remote --tags origin 'vstage*'` right before tagging, or the push fails with "already exists" and leaves a stale local tag to delete. Auto-review blocks tag pushes by policy; expect the approval card.
- 10:12 — Unquoted `: ` inside a plain YAML scalar (e.g. `disposition: ... probing: trx_class='CM'`) breaks `yaml.safe_load` with "mapping values are not allowed here". Parse the reply yaml before the user review, not at posting time.
- 10:12 — A `.xdm.catalog` archive's first CDATA is the 52-byte `<description>`; the SQL sits in the second CDATA, after `<sql dataSourceRef="...">` plus whitespace. `xdm_catalog_sql.py` slices the first CDATA only and returns the description.
