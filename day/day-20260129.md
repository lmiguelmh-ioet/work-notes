### Query to get mappings for style

```
SELECT 
    fdfcv.APPLICATION_COLUMN_NAME,
    fdfcv.END_USER_COLUMN_NAME,
    fdfcv.FORM_LEFT_PROMPT,
    fdfcv.COLUMN_SEQ_NUM,
    fdfcv.DESCRIPTION
FROM 
    fnd_descr_flex_col_usage_vl fdfcv
WHERE 
    fdfcv.DESCRIPTIVE_FLEXFIELD_NAME = 'EGO_ITEM_EFF'
    AND fdfcv.DESCRIPTIVE_FLEX_CONTEXT_CODE = 'All Items Attributes Style'
ORDER BY 
    fdfcv.COLUMN_SEQ_NUM;
```
