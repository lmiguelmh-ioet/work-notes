## Insights/Ideas
- `aws login` (console-credentials flow) rejects IAM Identity Center sessions — the authorize page 400s with "You may have typed the address incorrectly or you may have used an outdated link" — so SSO-backed access must use the Identity Center device flow instead. Also learned: the CLI has no `AWS_SSO_*` env vars (SSO config is config-file only), and `aws sso login` itself only requires `sso_start_url` + `sso_region` — account/role only matter for credential resolution, and `sso list-accounts` / `list-account-roles` (unsigned) can discover them from the token.
