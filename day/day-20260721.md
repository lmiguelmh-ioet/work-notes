Step 1: Create a SOAP Connection in OIC

1. Go to **Design** > **Connections** and click **Create**.
2. Search for and select **SOAP**.
3. Configure the details:
    - **Name:** `ERP_SOAP_Callback_Trigger`
    - **Role:** Select **Trigger**.
4. In the **Properties** panel:
    - **WSDL URL:** You need the standard Oracle Enterprise Scheduler (ESS) callback WSDL. Enter this exact URL (replace `<your-fusion-host>` with your actual Fusion domain):
        
        ```
        https://<your-fusion-host>/fscmService/JobNotificationService?XSD=JobNotificationService.xsd
        ```
        
5. In the **Security** panel:
    - **Security Policy:** Select **Username Password Token** (or **WS-Security UsernameToken** depending on your exact patch). This matches the exact token structure Fusion injects into that `<env:Body>` you shared.
6. Click **Test** (it will hit 100% since it's just a Trigger role) and click **Save**.

---

Step 2: Update Your Integration with the SOAP Trigger

1. Go back to your integration canvas (if it's active, deactivate it first).
2. Delete your old REST trigger node at the very beginning of the flow.
3. Click the `+` icon on the empty Start node and select your new **ERP_SOAP_Callback_Trigger** connection.
4. The configuration wizard will open:
    - **Select Port / Operation:** The WSDL will expose the **`onJobCompletion`** operation automatically. Select it.
    - Complete the wizard.

Now your integration canvas natively understands the exact XML structure you provided.
